   Rejection-rate bound (7). A chunk is certified-rejected iff
its certified margin is below threshold, ρ < . Because ρ
lower-bounds the true robustness by at most the tube radius
(the interval bound loses at most rH relative to the nominal

nominal trajectory), we have ρnom − rH ≤ ρ ≤ ρnom . Hence

bounded- density assumption. Adding gives α(H) ≤ α? +
β( + rH ).
